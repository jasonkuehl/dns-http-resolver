🔒 DNS Security Audit

Perform a comprehensive security audit of your domain's DNS configuration. Check for common misconfigurations and vulnerabilities.

💻 curl example 
curl "http://localhost:60200/api/security-audit?domain=example.com" | jq
This audit checks:
  • NS record redundancy
  • SOA record configuration
  • DNSSEC deployment
  • CAA records for certificate control
  • SPF email authentication
  • DMARC email policy
  • MX records
  • Zone transfer (AXFR) restrictions
