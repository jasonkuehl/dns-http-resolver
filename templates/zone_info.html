ℹ️ About Zone Info

This tool queries all common DNS record types (A, AAAA, NS, MX, TXT, SOA, CAA, CNAME, SRV, DNSKEY, DS) to provide a comprehensive overview of a domain's DNS configuration. It also detects common services based on TXT records (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, etc.).
